Privacy policy

Last updated: 31 October 2026. This policy explains what this website collects, why, and how to have it removed.

Who we are

This website is operated by Zhongshan Zuoyu Garment Co., Ltd., 6/F, No. 168, Yongheng Road, Qinggang Community, Dayong Town, Zhongshan, Guangdong, China. Business registration number shared on request. Contact for privacy matters: sales2zuoyu@aliyun.com.

What we collect

  • Information you send us. If you use the quote request form or email us, we receive the name, company, email address, quantity and brief you provide.
  • Basic technical logs. Our hosting provider records standard request data such as page requested, time, and the browser's user-agent string, for security and troubleshooting.
  • No advertising cookies. This site does not set advertising or cross-site tracking cookies. If site statistics are used, they are configured without cookies and report aggregate page views only.

Why we use it

To answer your enquiry, prepare a quotation, produce your order, and keep business records as required by law. We do not sell personal data, and we do not use it for automated decision-making.

Who we share it with

Only service providers needed to run this site and our business: the hosting provider, the email provider, and where applicable a shipping forwarder for a confirmed order. We do not publish client names or use customer data in marketing material without written permission.

How long we keep it

Enquiry records are kept for 3 years so that a later order can be quoted consistently. Invoices and shipping records are kept as long as accounting and customs rules require.

Your rights

You may ask for a copy of the personal data we hold about you, ask for correction, or ask for deletion. Write to sales2zuoyu@aliyun.com from the address you contacted us with. We respond within 30 days. If you are in the European Economic Area or the United Kingdom, you also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

International transfers

Our business is based in China, so data you send us is processed in China. Where a transfer restriction applies, we rely on your explicit request to receive a quotation as the basis for the transfer, or on a contractual safeguard with the recipient.

Security

The site is served over HTTPS. Access to enquiry data is limited to staff who need it to quote or produce an order. We do not accept payment card details through this website.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the "last updated" date above changes and the new version is published on this page.
