## What we make

Denim only: jeans, jackets, shirts and dresses — OEM and ODM for brand owners, importers
and e-commerce sellers in Europe, North America and Australia.

- Minimum order: 150 pieces per style per colour
- Sampling: 10-15 days, USD 100 per style, credited against the bulk invoice
- Bulk: 30-35 days after sample approval and deposit
- Incoterm: FOB Shenzhen

Certificates held: SMETA audit (Sedex reference ZAA600231250), US CPSIA General
Certificate of Conformity BYT26080342, RoHS certificate MTL24120600306R01.
Not held, and not claimed: OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS/RCS, amfori BSCI, ISO 9001.
