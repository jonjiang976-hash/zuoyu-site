Capabilities

What we do in-house, and what we do not

Manufacturing capability pages are usually a list of everything a supplier wishes it could do. This page is split into two columns on purpose: what runs under our own roof in Dayong, and what we buy in from neighbours in the cluster. Buyers who know which is which can plan their approval cycle correctly.

Short answer for buyers: pattern making, grading, sampling, cutting, sewing, in-house laundry and wash development, and final inspection are in-house. Fabric weaving, embroidery, screen printing and metal trims are purchased from long-standing suppliers in the Dayong cluster and managed by us.

Stage by stage

Production stages and ownership
Pattern making & gradingIn-house. A pattern-making team grades base blocks against your size chart. Base blocks are graded against your size chart; grading increments are confirmed before sampling.
Sample makingIn-house. Dedicated sample room with full-time machinists. First samples in 10–15 days, with a measurement report.
Fabric sourcingManaged, not milled by us. Denim is bought from mills in Guangdong against your required weight, composition and shade. We confirm the mill and the article before cutting.
CuttingIn-house. In-house cutting; marker making for fabric utilisation on multi-size orders.
SewingIn-house. 4 sewing lines, pieces per month across jeans, jackets, skirts and dresses.
Laundry & washIn-house. In-house laundry. Programme: stone wash, enzyme wash, laser whiskers and ozone bleaching. Sealed standards kept per order.
Laser & ozone finishingIn-house. Laser marking for whiskers and patterns; ozone and e-flow where the brief requires a lower-water finish.
Embroidery & printingOutsourced within the cluster. Managed by us, scheduled into the same production calendar.
Metal trimsOutsourced. Buttons, rivets and zips sourced to your reference; branded hardware requires tooling time.
Quality controlIn-house. Inline checks at sewing and wash stages; final AQL inspection with a written report.
Packing & shippingIn-house packing to your carton marking and packing list. FOB Shenzhen.

The wash programme

Wash is where denim orders are won or lost. Our programme covers stone wash, enzyme wash, laser whiskers and ozone bleaching. Each wash is developed against your approved reference, sealed, and then used for bulk — the sealed standard, not a photograph, is the acceptance criterion. Where a brief calls for a lower-impact finish, we propose laser and ozone routes instead of hand-sanding and note the cost and hand-feel trade-off in writing.

Cutting, pressing and final inspection — same site, same team

Sampling, stated as a process rather than a promise

  1. Brief review

    We read the tech pack for gaps before quoting. A missing measurement or an unstated trim is flagged in the quotation, not discovered in the sample.

  2. Quotation

    Costed quotation in 2 working days with the wash priced separately, so you can see what the finish costs.

  3. First sample

    10–15 days. Ships with a measurement report against your size chart and wash photographs under daylight.

  4. Fit and wash review

    You comment on measurements, not on "it feels off". We re-quote only if the construction changes.

  5. Approval and bulk

    Approved sample and sealed wash standard become the production reference. Bulk runs 30–35 days.

Quality control

Inline checks run at three points: after cutting, after sewing and after wash. Final inspection is AQL-based with a written report covering measurements, workmanship, wash match and packing. The report ships with the goods and is available to your buying team before the cartons leave.

Laundry on site

Stone, enzyme, laser and ozone work runs in our own laundry, a short walk from the sewing floor. That is what makes a wash trial a matter of days instead of a round trip: the person who approves your sample can stand next to the machine that runs it. It also means the sealed standard we keep for bulk is a sample we washed ourselves, not one we received from a third party.
