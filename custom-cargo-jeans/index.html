Custom cargo and utility jeans

Cargo denim adds cost in a place buyers do not expect: pockets. Each pocket is a separate pattern piece, a separate sewing operation and a place where the wash can catch. A utility jean with four pockets is not a jean with two extra bits of fabric - it is a different labour quote.

The style we produce, and the sewing floor it comes from

Short answer for buyers: send the pocket layout and the hardware you want; we quote the pocket work separately from the base jean so you can see what the utility detail costs.

Buyer questions
Do cargo pockets raise the minimum order?

No. The minimum stays at 150 pieces per style per colour; the pocket work changes the unit price, not the minimum.

Can you match a utility fabric that is not denim?

Yes - cotton twill and cotton-nylon blends are common on cargo styles. Send the swatch and we confirm the mill before quoting.

Which wash works best on cargo denim?

A vintage or monkey wash on 12-14 oz rigid denim holds structure and makes the pocket shading read. Very light washes tend to flatten the utility look.
