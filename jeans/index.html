What we need from you to quote: flat sketches or a reference sample, a measurement sheet (or a size chart to work from), fabric direction (rigid or stretch), wash reference photos, trim and label requirements, and your delivery window.

Why a pair of jeans can cost more than a jacket: our jacket listings are lightweight (10–12 oz) unisex styles at USD 16.57–18.67, while the standard men’s jeans are 12–14 oz rigid denim with a full wash programme, which carries more fabric, more laundry time and more panel matching. Heavier or lined jackets are quoted separately.

Wash development, not wash guessing

Jeans are judged on the wash. We develop the wash against an approved standard and keep a sealed reference for the order, so bulk is checked against the same physical sample instead of a photograph. If your brief is a vintage reproduction, expect the sample stage to carry most of the work — that is normal and it is priced into the sample, not into the bulk unit price.

Buyer questions on jeans
Can you copy a pair of jeans I already sell?

Usually, yes — send the pair plus your size chart. Copying a physical sample is faster than copying photos. If the garment uses a proprietary fabric or hardware, we will tell you where the match will differ.

Do you grade plus sizes?

Yes, plus-size grading is available. Send the base size and the target size run; we will confirm the grading increments in the quotation.

Can you do laser patterns and hand distressing?

Yes. Our wash programme includes stone wash, enzyme wash, laser whiskers and ozone bleaching, and distressing can be specified per panel or per placement. Send your reference images and we will quote the placement work separately from the base wash.
