2. Denim weight: oz/yd² to g/m²

One ounce per square yard is 33.906 g/m². Convert either way; the table shows what each weight is actually used for.

Typical denim sits between 8 and 16 oz. The same 12 oz fabric feels softer after a heavy wash — weight sets the starting stiffness, not the finish.

How we grade: we cut to your size chart, not to ours. These tables are the starting point we quote from and the range we can grade across without re-blocking. If your chart sits between two rows, we grade to yours — the tables simply say what the base block covers.

How these numbers are defined

Consumption is net panel area divided by (usable width × marker efficiency), multiplied by one plus the end-loss and defect allowance. It is the same method we use when quoting small orders, so the output is comparable with a quotation rather than a textbook figure.

Weight uses the standard conversion of 1 oz/yd² = 33.906 g/m². Denim class ranges are the ones we see across the styles we run, not a formal standard.

Container capacity is the commonly quoted usable volume (20GP 28 CBM, 40GP 58 CBM, 40HQ 68 CBM) at 85% fill. Confirm with your forwarder before booking; we are not a freight forwarder.

These calculators are indicative. A quotation against your tech pack or reference garment is the only number that holds.
