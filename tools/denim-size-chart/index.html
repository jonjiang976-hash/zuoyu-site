How the base blocks are graded

One base pattern per category, graded up and down from it. The increments below are what our pattern room uses; the tolerances are what we hold the sealed sample to before bulk.

How to measure

  • Chest / bust — around the fullest part, tape level under the arms, arms down.
  • Waist — at the narrowest point of the torso, measured on the body rather than over a waistband.
  • Hip — around the fullest part of the seat, roughly 18–20 cm below the waist.
  • Inseam — from the crotch seam to the desired hem, standing without shoes.
  • Back length — centre back neck point down to the hem, without stretching the fabric.

Size chart questions

What size chart does a Chinese denim factory grade from?

We grade from our own base blocks and publish the EU, US, UK and Japan conversions with body measurements in centimetres for six categories: women's tops, trousers, shorts and skirts, and men's tops and jeans. Letter sizes have no official international definition, so a brand's own size chart overrides ours.

Why does the same letter size fit differently between brands?

Letter sizes are not standardised and each brand shifts its base pattern. Compare the body measurements in centimetres with your own size chart rather than the letter.

Do you cut to our size chart or yours?

We cut to yours. These tables are the starting point we quote from and the range we can grade across without re-blocking. If your chart sits between two rows, we grade to yours.

What is the grading tolerance?

About 0.5 cm on body measurements and 0.3 cm on shoulder width, measured on the sealed approved sample before bulk. If the sample measures outside tolerance, we re-cut before production.
