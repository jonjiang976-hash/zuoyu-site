Custom maxi and long denim skirt

A maxi skirt is a denim dress problem in a skirt shape: long panels, a heavy hem and a wash that has to stay even from waist to floor. It is also one of the few denim categories where a single style can carry a whole summer range, so the first sample matters more than the price.

The style we produce, and the sewing floor it comes from

Short answer for buyers: send the length, slit position and belt detail you want; we confirm the fabric weight and lining, quote in 2 working days, and sample in 10-15 days.

Buyer questions
How much does a custom maxi denim skirt cost?

USD 13.97-19.12 per piece FOB Shenzhen for the listed styles, depending on fabric weight, lining and wash. MOQ is 150 pieces per style per colour.

Can a maxi skirt share a wash with my jeans?

Yes - if both are cut from the same fabric lot and washed together. That is how a coordinated set stays matched instead of being matched after the fact.

Does a long skirt need a lining?

For summer weights, usually not; for heavier 10-12 oz denim, a half-lining makes it wearable. We quote both so the choice is visible.
