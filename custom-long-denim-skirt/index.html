Custom long straight denim skirt

A long straight skirt is a pattern and wash problem more than a sewing problem: the slit has to sit where the leg moves, and a dark wash has to stay even from waistband to hem. Get those two right and the style sells for several seasons without a change.

Both angles of the style we produce, and the sewing floor it comes from

Short answer for buyers: send the slit position and length you want plus a wash reference; we confirm fabric weight and lining, quote in 2 working days, and sample in 10-15 days.

Buyer questions
Where should the slit sit on a long denim skirt?

Where the leg actually moves: front-centre for walking, side for a straighter line. We agree the slit height against a physical sample because two centimetres changes how the skirt walks.

Can the skirt be unlined?

For 9-10 oz denim, usually yes. For heavier 11-12 oz, a half-lining makes it wearable in summer. Both are quoted so the choice is visible.

Can a long skirt share a wash with my jeans?

Yes, if both are cut from the same fabric lot and washed together - that is how a matched set stays matched.
