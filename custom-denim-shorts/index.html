Custom denim shorts

Shorts are the lowest-cost denim product per piece and the fastest to sell out - which makes them the right first style for a new brand. The two decisions that matter are the inseam (it moves more than buyers expect between sizes) and the hem: a clean hem and a frayed hem are different operations, not a toggle.

The style we produce, and the sewing floor it comes from

Short answer for buyers: send the reference short or a tech pack with the inseam and hem finish; we quote in 2 working days and cut a first sample in 10-15 days.

Buyer questions
How much does a custom denim short cost?

USD 12.50-14.50 per piece FOB Shenzhen for the listed women's styles, depending on fabric, wash and hem finish. It is the lowest price band in our range.

Can you make a frayed hem that does not keep fraying?

Yes. Fraying is controlled by the cut, the wash and a lock-stitch bar; we agree the level of fray against a physical sample so bulk matches it.

What is the simplest first order?

One short, one wash, 150 pieces. It is the cheapest way to test a fit and a supplier before committing a full collection.
