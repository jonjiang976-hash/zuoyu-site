Buyer FAQ

Questions buyers ask before the first order

Twelve questions, answered with numbers instead of adjectives. Where the honest answer is "no", it says no.

If you only read one answer: the minimum order is 150 pieces per style per colour, a first sample takes 10–15 days, bulk takes 30–35 days, and we are SMETA audited with GCC and RoHS — we do not hold OEKO-TEX, GRS or BSCI.

What is the minimum order quantity for custom denim?

150 pieces per style per colour. The per-colour minimum is quoted with the style; adding a colour to an existing style does not reset the style minimum. The per-colour minimum is quoted with the style — we do not hide a colour minimum inside the style minimum.

How much do custom jeans cost?

USD 18–26 per piece FOB Shenzhen, depending on fabric weight, wash and trims. Denim jackets start at USD 16.57–18.67, skirts at USD 14–19 and dresses at USD 14–19. Wash development is priced separately in the quotation so you can see what the finish costs.

How long does a sample take?

10–15 days for a first sample, shipped with a measurement report against your size chart and daylight wash photographs. The sample fee is USD 100 per style and is credited against the bulk invoice.

How long does bulk production take?

30–35 days after sample approval and deposit. Weekly photo updates are sent at cutting, sewing and wash stages.

Are you GRS or BSCI certified?

No. We do not hold OEKO-TEX Standard 100. We are SMETA audited and hold US CPSIA GCC and RoHS certificates. We do not hold GRS, amfori BSCI or ISO 9001, and we do not claim them. If recycled content or a BSCI report is mandatory for your programme, tell us before the quotation — we would rather lose the quotation than the deposit.

Do you hold OEKO-TEX certification?

No. We do not hold OEKO-TEX Standard 100, and we will not describe denim as OEKO-TEX certified. We are SMETA audited (ZAA600231250) and hold US CPSIA GCC (BYT26080342) and RoHS (MTL24120600306R01). If your programme requires OEKO-TEX, tell us before quotation.

Who owns the patterns and designs?

You do. Patterns, graded sizes and artwork developed for your order belong to you. We do not resell your designs or produce them for other clients.

Can you produce from an existing sample instead of a tech pack?

Yes, usually. Sending the physical garment plus your size chart is faster and more accurate than sending photographs. If the garment uses a proprietary fabric or custom hardware, we will tell you where the match will differ.

What are your payment terms?

30% deposit with the order and 70% before shipment. We do not start bulk production without a deposit, and we never accept deposit payments to a personal account.

What does FOB mean here, and which port do you ship from?

FOB Shenzhen: we deliver the goods cleared for export on board at the named port, and the buyer pays freight and insurance from there. If you need DDP or delivery to an Amazon FBA warehouse, ask for a separate quotation — it is a different commercial arrangement.

How do I know you are a real factory?

Ask for the business registration number and check the registered company name against the bank beneficiary on the invoice. Ask for the OEKO-TEX certificate number and the SMETA report reference and verify them yourself. Ask for a live video call on the production floor. Then place a paid sample order before bulk — a sample is the cheapest verification a buyer can buy.

Do you work with independent designers and small brands?

Yes, and they are a large part of our order book. The practical advice for a first collection is to order fewer styles at the minimum order quantity rather than many styles below it: the cost per piece is better and the wash approval cycle is shorter.

Still unanswered? Send the question — we answer within 1 working day. If the answer is "no", we will say no rather than quote around it.
