The fit risk, stated plainly

Denim dresses fail on fit more often than on wash. Rigid denim does not forgive a waist measurement the way a knit does, and a shirt dress that reads well on a flat will read differently on a body. Our first sample round therefore includes a measurement report and a form-fitting check against your size chart, and we tell you which measurements we expect to change before you invest in a second round. If a design needs three sample rounds, we will say so at quotation — not after you have paid for three.

Buyer questions on dresses
Can you work from a sketch rather than a tech pack?

Yes, for a first quotation. A sketch plus a size chart gets you a price band. A full tech pack with measurements and construction notes is needed before sampling, because sampling without it produces a sample nobody can approve.

Do you handle lining and pockets?

Yes — lining direction and pocket bag construction are specified in the tech pack. If a design is unlined, we will flag where a facing or binding is needed to keep the inside clean.

Can a dress share a wash with my jeans?

Only if the fabric article and weight allow it. Dresses usually run at lighter weights than 5-pocket jeans, so the wash is developed separately unless the articles match. We will tell you at quotation whether a shared wash is realistic.
