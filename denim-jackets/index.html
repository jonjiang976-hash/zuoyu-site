Why jackets cost more than their fabric

A jacket has more pattern pieces than a pair of jeans, and the two front panels have to read as one garment after washing. The wash pulls the fabric differently across a shoulder seam than across a leg seam, so the sealed standard is checked at the yoke and the pocket flap, not only on a flat swatch.

Hardware is the second cost driver. If you want a branded shank button, that is a tooling order before it is a garment order; if the design can use a stock button with a custom logo plate, the tooling cost drops and the lead time shortens. We quote both routes so the difference is visible before you commit.

Buyer questions on jackets
Can you copy a jacket I already sell?

Yes, usually — send the jacket plus your size chart. A physical sample is faster and more accurate than photographs, especially for the yoke shape and the chest pocket placement.

Do you make custom-branded buttons and rivets?

Yes. Branded shank buttons, snap buttons and rivets are tooled to order: expect 15–25 days of tooling lead time and a separate tooling cost, quoted before sampling. Stock hardware with a custom logo plate is the faster route.

Can you produce a jacket and skirt set in the same wash?

Yes. Sets are cut and washed as a pair so the two pieces match; see the jacket and skirt sets page for how the approval works.
