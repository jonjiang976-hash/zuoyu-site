Evidence

Factory, audits, and what we do not hold

Every claim on this page is written so a buyer can check it: a certificate number you can query, an audit reference your compliance team can look up, and a plain list of the certifications we do not have.

Short answer for buyers: we are a denim factory in Dayong, Zhongshan, Guangdong, with four sewing lines and 2,300 m² of production and warehouse space. We are SMETA audited (Sedex reference ZAA600231250) and hold US CPSIA GCC and RoHS certificates for our denim. We do not hold OEKO-TEX Standard 100. We do not hold GRS, amfori BSCI or ISO 9001 — if your programme requires any of these, tell us before the quotation.

Certification status

Held and not held
OEKO-TEX Standard 100Not held. We do not hold this certificate, and we do not describe any article as OEKO-TEX certified. Buyers who need it should source from a supplier that lists the specific articles on a valid certificate.
SMETA (Sedex)Audited. Audit report (CAPR) reference ZAA600231250 on file; the full report is shared on request for vendor onboarding rather than published, because it contains site-level corrective actions. SMETA is a social and ethical audit methodology, not a product certification.

GCC (US CPSIA)Held. General Certificate of Conformity for denim jeans, models ZY001–ZY030. Test report BYT26080342; manufacture date 20 August 2026. This is the document US customs and marketplace compliance teams ask for.
RoHS 2.0Held. Certificate MTL24120600306R01 for denim jeans, model ZY2480, issued 16 December 2024 to Directive 2011/65/EU. Relevant to trims and hardware rather than the fabric itself — we state the scope rather than implying the denim is 'RoHS certified'.
Sedex membershipRegistered on Sedex; buyers can request access to our site record. Membership does not equal a higher-tier audit — check the audit type on the record.

How to verify us before you pay a deposit

  1. Ask for the OEKO-TEX certificate number and check the certificate type and the covered articles, not just the logo.
  2. Ask for the SMETA report reference and request access to the Sedex record directly through the platform.
  3. Ask for our business registration number and the registered English company name, and check that they match the bank beneficiary on the invoice.
  4. Ask for a live video call on the production floor. We do not accept a recorded video as factory verification and we do not ask you to accept one from us.
  5. Place a paid sample order before bulk. A sample is the cheapest verification available to a buyer.

Why this section exists: most denim suppliers list certifications they cannot produce on request. We would rather lose a quotation at the certification question than lose a customer at the inspection.

Safety drills

Fire drills and evacuation practice run with the whole site, not just the office: alarm, stairwell evacuation, assembly and roll call in the yard, then extinguisher practice. Health and safety is one of the areas a SMETA audit examines.
