04 — Compliance

What we can prove — and what we cannot

Most denim suppliers online claim every certification. Buyers have learned to discount that. Here is our position, written so it can be checked.

Certification status
OEKO-TEX Standard 100 Not held. We do not hold OEKO-TEX Standard 100. What we can evidence instead: SMETA audit (ZAA600231250), US CPSIA GCC (BYT26080342) and RoHS (MTL24120600306R01).
SMETA audit (Sedex) Audited. SMETA audit report reference ZAA600231250 on file, shared on request. SMETA is a social-compliance audit methodology, not a product certification — we describe it that way on purpose.
GRS / RCS Not held. We do not have recycled-content certification. If your brief requires GRS, we will tell you at quotation stage instead of after the deposit.
BSCI / amfori Not held. We are SMETA audited rather than BSCI audited. If your buying team requires an amfori BSCI report, that is a real gap and we will say so.
ISO 9001 Not held. Quality control is documented per order through our own AQL inspection records.

Product-level documents: US CPSIA General Certificate of Conformity (report BYT26080342) and RoHS certificate MTL24120600306R01 — both scans are on the factory page.

05 — How an order runs

From tech pack to shipment, in five steps

  1. Brief and quotation

    Send a tech pack, a reference sample or even annotated photos. Within 2 working days you receive a costed quotation with the wash option priced separately.

  2. Sampling

    First samples in 10–15 days. We send measurement reports and wash photos with the sample so you can review against your size chart before shipping it back and forth.

  3. Wash and fit confirmation

    Wash trials are developed against your approved standard. We keep a sealed standard for every order so bulk is checked against the same reference.

  4. Bulk production

    30–35 days after approval and deposit. Weekly photo updates at cutting, sewing and wash stages.

  5. Inspection and shipment

    Final AQL inspection, packing list and carton marking to your specification. FOB Shenzhen.

06 — Order scenarios

Three briefs we quote most often

No client names, no logos — those belong to our customers. What we can publish is the brief, the constraint and the outcome.

Scenario 01

First collection, four styles. A brand owner sends four tech packs at the 150-piece minimum. The step that decides the timeline is wash approval, not sewing — so the wash is quoted and sampled as its own line item, and the bulk date starts once the sealed standard is signed.

Scenario 02

Jacket and skirt, one wash. A matched set needs one wash standard across two garments. Because both run through the same laundry on site, the set is developed and approved as a pair instead of being matched after the fact from two different suppliers.

Scenario 03

Reproducing a vintage wash. A buyer sends a worn garment and asks for the same fade. We rebuild it from fabric weight, wash recipe and laser placement, then keep the approved sample as the sealed standard the bulk is checked against.

07 — Buyer questions

The four questions we are asked before every order

What is your MOQ, and is it per style or per colour?

150 pieces per style. The per-colour minimum is quoted with the style; adding a colour to an existing style does not reset the style minimum. If your range needs several colours, the per-colour minimum is quoted with the style — we will not hide a colour minimum inside the style minimum.

Can you make a sample from my tech pack before I commit to bulk?

Yes. Sampling is a separate paid step and costs USD 100 per style. First samples take 10–15 days. The sample fee is credited against your bulk invoice when the order is placed.

Do you hold GRS or BSCI certification?

No. We do not hold OEKO-TEX Standard 100. We are SMETA audited and hold US CPSIA GCC and RoHS certificates. We do not claim GRS or BSCI. If recycled content or an amfori BSCI report is mandatory for your programme, say so early — we would rather lose the quotation than the deposit.

Who owns the patterns and the prints?

You do. Patterns, graded sizes and artwork developed for your order are yours; we do not resell your designs or produce them for other clients.
