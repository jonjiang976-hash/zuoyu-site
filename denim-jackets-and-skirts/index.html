Custom denim jackets and denim skirts

Trucker jackets, overshirts, chore jackets and denim skirts — including coordinated jacket-and-skirt sets for resort and young-contemporary ranges. 150 pieces per style per colour, from USD 16.50–23.50 per piece FOB Shenzhen.

Jacket, overshirt and set programmes, washed against one sealed standard

Short answer for buyers: the outerwear and skirt line shares the same wash floor as our jeans, so a coordinated set can be washed and confirmed together — one wash standard, one approval, one shipment.

Why sets are easier here than with a trading company

A coordinated set fails in one of two ways: the jacket and the skirt come out of different washes, or they come out of different factories and arrive in different shades. Because jacket, skirt and jean production all pass through the same wash floor, the set is washed together and approved against one standard. That is the part a trading company cannot reproduce.

Buyer questions on jackets and skirts
Can the jacket and skirt be washed as a matched pair?

Yes. Set orders are washed together and approved against one sealed standard. Tell us at quotation stage that the two pieces must match — it changes the wash plan, not the production line.

Do you make heavyweight 14 oz trucker jackets?

Yes. Rigid 12–14 oz trucker jackets are a standard construction. Heavier weights are possible but the fabric is confirmed per order with the mill.

What about small runs for a first collection?

The minimum is 150 pieces per style per colour. For a first collection we usually recommend fewer styles at the minimum rather than many styles below it — the cost per piece is better and the wash approval is faster.
