Denim glossary for buyers

The terms that decide whether a quotation is comparable: what an ounce weight means, what "sanforised" promises, and which certifications are product certifications versus audits. Written so a definition can be quoted on its own.

Start here: MOQ is the minimum per style and per colour; FOB means the buyer pays freight after the port; OEKO-TEX Standard 100 certifies listed articles, while SMETA audits the factory's labour and ethics practices. Those three distinctions cause more quoting errors than any technical term on this page.

Fabric and construction

Denim weight (oz)
Fabric weight in ounces per square yard. 9–11 oz is light shirting and dress denim; 12–14 oz is standard 5-pocket jeans; 14 oz and above is heavyweight workwear.
Sanforised
Pre-shrunk in finishing so the garment shrinks under roughly 1% on first wash. Un-sanforised (raw) denim can shrink 3% or more — a size chart for raw denim should state the shrink allowance.
Whiskers
Faded creases radiating from the front hip, formed by wear and reproduced in production by laser, hand-sanding or both.
Honeycomb
The faded pattern behind the knee, usually reproduced with laser or sanding against the sealed wash standard.
Sealed standard
A buyer-approved physical sample kept on site. Bulk is checked against it — not against a photograph.

Wash and finishing

Laser wash
Dry process that burns the fade pattern into the denim. Repeatable, and lower in water use than hand processing.
Ozone wash
Bleaching with ozone gas instead of hypochlorite or a large water bath. Used to lift shade while cutting water use per garment.
e-flow
Low-water finishing that applies treatment as a foam rather than a bath, reducing wastewater against conventional garment washing.
Stone wash
Pumice stones abrade the garment for a softer hand-feel and faded look.
Enzyme wash
Cellulase enzymes soften denim and remove surface fuzz without stones — a cleaner finish.
Acid wash
Pumice plus chlorine for a high-contrast mottled finish. Rarely specified now for environmental reasons.

Ordering and commercial terms

Tech pack
Flat sketches, construction, measurements, fabric, wash reference, trims, labelling and packaging. A complete tech pack is the difference between an accurate quotation and a guess.
MOQ
Minimum Order Quantity. Here: 150 pieces per style per colour. Conventions differ between suppliers — always ask whether the minimum is per order, per style or per colour.
AQL
Acceptable Quality Limit: the sampling standard behind a final inspection report, which usually states the level applied.
FOB
Free On Board. Goods are cleared for export and loaded at the named port; the buyer pays freight and insurance from there. Our quotations are FOB Shenzhen.
DDP
Delivered Duty Paid: delivery to the buyer's address with duties paid. A different risk allocation, priced separately.

Certification terms, precisely

OEKO-TEX Standard 100
A product-level certification for restricted substances on the articles and materials listed on the certificate. It is not a company-wide certification and it does not cover production ethics.
SMETA
Sedex Members Ethical Trade Audit: a social and ethical audit covering labour standards, health and safety, environment and business ethics. It is an audit methodology — there is no such thing as "SMETA certified".
GRS
Global Recycled Standard for recycled-content product claims, covering material share and chain of custody. We do not hold GRS, so we do not claim recycled denim.
BSCI
amfori BSCI: a social compliance audit framework used widely by European retailers. We are SMETA audited instead.

Why the wording matters: if a supplier writes "SMETA certified" or "BSCI certified", the phrase itself is wrong — those are audit schemes, not certifications. Checking that wording is a fast way to tell whether the supplier wrote the page or copied it.
