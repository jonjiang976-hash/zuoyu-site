What makes a quotation accurate

  • Flat sketches, or a reference garment
  • Size chart and target size run
  • Fabric direction: rigid or stretch, and approximate weight
  • Wash reference photos, or a sample
  • Trim and labelling requirements
  • Target quantity per style and per colour
  • Delivery window and destination port / warehouse

Missing most of that? Send the reference garment and the quantity. We can quote a band from that, and we will say which assumptions the band depends on.
