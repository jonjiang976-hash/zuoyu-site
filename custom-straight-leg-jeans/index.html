Custom straight-leg jeans with patchwork panels

Straight-leg is the volume block, and patchwork is what makes a straight jean worth a second look. The hard part is not the pattern, it is matching the panels after wash: two fabrics that read the same in the cutting room can separate after 30 minutes in the drum. We develop the wash across the assembled garment, not on a flat swatch.

The style we produce, and the sewing floor it comes from

Short answer for buyers: send the panel layout (or the garment), the fabric for each panel and your size chart; we quote in 2 working days and build the first sample in 10-15 days with the wash already developed on the assembled jean.

Buyer questions
Will patchwork panels align after washing?

They will if each fabric is pre-tested for shrinkage and the wash is developed on the assembled garment. That is why we ask for the panel layout before quoting rather than after.

Can the panels be different denim weights?

Yes, but expect a separate shrinkage test and a second wash trial. Mixing weights is a design decision with a cost, and we quote it as one.

What is the minimum for a patchwork style?

150 pieces per style per colour, the same as any other style. The construction does not raise the minimum.
