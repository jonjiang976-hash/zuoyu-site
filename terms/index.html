Terms of use and sales terms

Last updated: 31 October 2026. These terms cover use of this website and outline the commercial terms that apply to orders. A confirmed order is governed by the signed quotation and any sales contract issued with it.

1. Use of this website

The content on this site is provided for general information about our manufacturing services. Prices, lead times and specifications shown are indicative bands, not offers. Nothing on this site constitutes a binding offer to supply goods.

2. Quotations

Quotations are prepared against the brief supplied and are valid for 30 days unless stated otherwise. A quotation assumes the specifications in the brief; changes to fabric, wash, trims or quantity are re-quoted.

3. Sampling

Sampling is a paid step at USD 100 per style, credited against the bulk invoice for the same style. Samples remain our property until the bulk order is placed unless agreed otherwise in writing. Sample lead time is 10–15 days from receipt of a complete tech pack and any required reference materials.

4. Orders, deposits and production

Bulk production starts after written approval of the sample and receipt of the agreed deposit. Payment terms are 30% deposit with the order and 70% before shipment. Bulk lead time runs from the later of sample approval and deposit receipt, and is 30–35 days unless the quotation states otherwise. Delays caused by late buyer-supplied materials, artwork or approvals extend the lead time accordingly.

5. Intellectual property

Designs, patterns, graded sizes and artwork developed for a buyer remain the buyer's property. The buyer confirms that any design, artwork or trademark supplied to us does not infringe a third party's rights. We do not produce a buyer's design for another client.

6. Specifications and tolerances

Garment measurements are subject to normal manufacturing tolerances, stated in the approved specification. Colour and wash are matched to the buyer-approved sealed standard under standard lighting; a match to a photograph is not the acceptance criterion.

7. Inspection and claims

Goods are inspected before shipment and a written inspection report is issued. Claims for defects visible on the inspection report are resolved before shipment. Claims raised after delivery must be notified within 7 days of receipt, with photographs and the inspection report reference. Claims do not cover normal variation within the approved tolerance.

8. Delivery and risk

Quotations are FOB Shenzhen unless stated otherwise. Under FOB terms, risk passes when the goods are loaded on board at the named port. DDP or delivered terms are quoted separately.

9. Certification references

We are SMETA audited and hold US CPSIA GCC and RoHS certificates. We do not hold OEKO-TEX Standard 100, GRS, amfori BSCI or ISO 9001. A buyer who cites our certification in their own product claims must check the covered articles on the certificate first.

10. Force majeure and governing law

Neither party is liable for delay caused by events outside its reasonable control. These terms are governed by the laws of the People's Republic of China; disputes are referred to the competent court at the place of our registration unless the sales contract provides for arbitration.
