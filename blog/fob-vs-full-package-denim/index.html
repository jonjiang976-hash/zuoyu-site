FOB and Full Package Are Not the Same Thing: A Denim Buyer's Guide

Buyers ask "do you do FOB or full package?" as if these were two routes to the same place. They are not even the same kind of thing. FOB describes where your responsibility starts; full package describes how much of the work the factory does. You can be FOB and full package at the same time, and most of our first-time buyers are.

The difference in one line

FOB is an Incoterm: the factory delivers the goods cleared for export onto the ship at the named port, and you pay freight and import from there. Full package is a service scope: the factory sources fabric and trims, makes the pattern, produces, finishes, packs and hands you a boxed, label-ready product.

So the question is not "which one", it is two separate questions:

  1. Who books and pays the freight? — that is the shipping term.
  2. Who does design, sourcing, pattern, sampling, production and packing? — that is the service scope.

What each side of the line covers

ItemWho does what
Fabric and trimsYou: nominate the mill and approve the article · Factory: sources, tests and confirms the article
Pattern and gradingYou: supply the tech pack · Factory: makes the pattern and grades from your size chart
SamplingYou: approve or reject · Factory: develops the sample and the wash
ProductionFactory only: cutting, sewing, finishing, pressing
WashYou: approve the standard · Factory: develops and seals the wash
Labels and packagingYou: supply the design and often the labels · Factory: sources or applies labels, polybags and cartons
InspectionYou: can book a third party · Factory: runs AQL and reports
Freight and importYou - still yours unless the term changes to CIF or DDP
Duty and clearanceYou - still yours

The important part: the last two rows stay yours even on a full package order, because the shipping term is decided separately.

What full package actually changes

It changes the number of people you have to manage. On a pure FOB order with your own fabric and trims, you are coordinating a mill, a trim supplier, a label printer and a factory — four calendars. On full package, that becomes one invoice and one delivery date, at the cost of a service margin inside the unit price.

It also changes where errors get caught. When the factory sources the fabric, the factory owns the shade standard; when you nominate the mill, a shade mismatch is a conversation between three parties.

What full package does not include

Three things buyers assume are included and usually are not:

  • Freight, insurance and duty. Those follow the shipping term, not the service scope.
  • Third-party inspection or testing. AQL inspection from the factory is standard; an SGS or Bureau Veritas report is a separate booking.
  • Design. Full package means the factory can execute a design; it does not mean the factory invents the collection.

Which model fits which team

Your situationBetter fit
You have a designer and a tech pack, and you have shipped beforeFOB with nominated fabric — you keep control of the article and the shade
You have a design but no sourcing contactsFull package — one counterpart, one delivery

How we quote it

Our default is FOB Shenzhen with the fabric and trims quoted as their own lines, so you can see what a nominated article would change. When a buyer wants full package we quote the same garment with sourcing and packing folded in, and we still show freight as a separate line because it is not ours to quote.

If you are not sure which model you are being quoted, that is the first thing to settle — the price difference between the two is mostly the coordination you are buying back.

Buyer questions
Is FOB the same as full package?

No. FOB is a shipping term that sets where your responsibility starts; full package is a service scope that sets how much of the work the factory does. A factory can quote FOB and still supply a full package service.

What does full package include in garment manufacturing?

Fabric and trim sourcing, pattern making and grading, sampling, production, wash development, labels and packing, and in-house AQL inspection. Freight, duty and third-party testing are normally outside it.

Which is cheaper, FOB or full package?

Per piece, FOB with your own fabric often looks cheaper because you buy the fabric directly. In total cost, full package is frequently cheaper for a first order because it removes the coordination and the mistakes of managing four suppliers.

Does full package include shipping to my country?

Not by itself. Shipping follows the Incoterm: if you want the factory to handle freight you need CIF or DDP terms quoted separately, and duty is still yours under most of them.
