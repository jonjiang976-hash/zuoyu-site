Custom denim jacket manufacturer: what MOQ 150 actually means

A minimum order quantity is usually quoted as a single number and explained as a sales policy. It is neither. Here is what our 150-piece minimum covers, what it blocks, and how it changes the way you plan a first denim jacket collection.

Short answer: our minimum is 150 pieces per style, per colour. One jacket style in one colour is 150 pieces. One style in two colours is 300. Four styles in two colours is 1,200. The number comes from fabric roll minimums, cutting marker efficiency and wash batch size — not from a sales target.

What 150 pieces covers

Order sizes at the minimum
1 style × 1 colour150 pcs
1 style × 2 colours300 pcs
2 styles × 2 colours600 pcs
4 styles × 2 colours1,200 pcs

Size ratios do not change the minimum. A 150-piece order split across S–XL is still one style and one colour; you are not charged extra for grading the size run.

Why the minimum exists

Three production realities set it, and all three are physical:

  • Fabric minimums. Denim is woven in rolls. A short run of a specific weight and shade still consumes a roll minimum, so the fabric cost per piece rises sharply below the threshold.
  • Cutting efficiency. A marker is laid across the fabric to cut multiple sizes at once. Below the minimum, the marker wastes fabric and the cut becomes uneconomical for both sides.
  • Wash batch size. Denim is finished in machine batches. A batch smaller than the minimum either wastes capacity or forces your order into a shared batch, which makes shade consistency harder to guarantee.

Hardware adds a smaller constraint: custom buttons, rivets and zip pulls have their own tooling and minimum quantities. If your design uses proprietary hardware, that minimum is quoted separately and can exceed the garment minimum.

What 150 pieces means for a first collection

The practical consequence is a planning rule we give almost every first-time buyer: fewer styles at the minimum beats many styles below it. Four styles at 150 pieces each gives you a coherent range, a better unit price and one wash approval cycle per style. Twelve styles at 50 pieces each gives you neither the price nor the speed.

What the minimum does not get you

It is worth being equally clear about the other direction. A 150-piece order does not buy you a bespoke fabric development, a dedicated wash recipe for a single colour, or a new hardware tooling. Those are separate projects with their own minimums, and we will say so at quotation rather than discover it during sampling.

Buyer questions
Can I split the 150 pieces across two colours?

Yes, but the minimum applies per colour. Two colours start at 300 pieces. The per-colour minimum is quoted with the style, so you see it before sampling.

Is the sample fee credited against the bulk order?

Yes. Sampling is USD 100 per style and is credited against the bulk invoice for the same style once the order is placed.

What if my first order is below 150 pieces?

We will tell you at quotation stage rather than after a deposit. For a first collection the usual answer is fewer styles at the minimum instead of many styles below it: the unit price is lower and the wash approval cycle is shorter.
