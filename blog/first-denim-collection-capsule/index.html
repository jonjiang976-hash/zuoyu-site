Your First Denim Collection: A Six-Style Capsule at 900 Pieces

A first denim collection usually fails for one of two reasons: too many styles, or the wrong six. At 150 pieces per style per colour, a six-style range is 900 pieces — enough to test a fit, a wash and a market without betting the season on one silhouette. This is the sequence we see work.

Why six styles, not sixteen

Six is the number where a range still reads as a range and the buyer can still afford to be wrong about two of them. A sixteen-style first collection spreads the same money across 2,400 pieces, which means every style is under-sampled, every wash is under-tested, and nothing is deep enough to reorder.

The other reason is production. Six styles share fabric lots, share wash drum loads and share the same sewing line time. That is where the cost advantage of a capsule comes from — not from the price per piece.

What 900 pieces actually costs

At the bands above, the garment cost of a six-style capsule sits roughly between USD 12,900 and USD 17,500 FOB for 900 pieces, before wash development, tooling and freight. Two things move that number:

  • Wash complexity. A one-step enzyme wash and a five-step vintage reproduction on the same jean are not the same product. Wash is quoted as its own line for exactly this reason.
  • Tooling. Custom buttons, rivets or a branded label are one-off costs. They change the quote once, not the price per piece.

Shipping, duty and any third-party testing sit outside that estimate and should be priced separately before you commit.

Sample in this order, not all at once

  1. The straight or wide-leg jean first. It is the fit the rest of the range will be measured against, and it is the fastest to correct.
  2. The jacket second. It shares a wash standard with the jeans and reveals whether the fabric reads the same on a different panel size.
  3. The shorts third. Same fabric family, cheaper per piece, quick sanity check on the hem and the wash level.
  4. Cargo and skirt last. Both have construction work (pockets, slits) that is easier to judge once the base fits are approved.

Sampling runs 10–15 days per style with the fee credited against the bulk invoice, so three rounds of two styles is usually faster than one round of six.

Test, then deepen

A 900-piece capsule is not a season, it is a test. The three numbers to watch in the first month are sell-through per style, return rate per fit, and which wash photographs best in your own content. Whichever two styles lead, order those deeper next time and drop the weakest one. That is how a six-style capsule becomes a permanent range with a reorder history behind it.

Buyer questions
What is the minimum order for a first denim collection?

150 pieces per style per colour. A six-style capsule is therefore 900 pieces, and the only way to go lower is fewer styles, not fewer pieces per style.

Which styles should a first denim brand start with?

Straight or wide-leg jeans, one utility or cargo style, denim shorts, a jacket and a skirt or dress. That mix covers the volume block, the trend story and a non-jeans option.

How long does a first collection take from sample to delivery?

Sampling is 10-15 days per style, bulk is 30-35 days after approval and deposit. A six-style capsule with two sampling rounds usually lands 8-11 weeks after the first brief.

Can I split the 900 pieces across colours?

Only if each colour meets the per-colour minimum of 150 pieces. Two colours on six styles means 1,800 pieces, which is why most first capsules start with one colour per style.
