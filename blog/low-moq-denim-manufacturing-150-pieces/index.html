Low MOQ Denim Manufacturing: 150 Pieces

At 150 pieces per style per colour, a first order buys one colourway in a full size run — not two colours, not four styles in depth. The number is set by fabric dye lots, cutting markers and wash batch size, not by how fast the sewing line runs.

This page is about what that 150 buys you in combinations and cost. If you want the per-style meaning of the minimum instead, that is covered in what MOQ 150 actually means.

What 150 pieces actually covers

150 pieces is one style, one colour, one size run. A workable curve for a women's jean:

XS10 pcs · 7% of the order
S25 pcs · 17%
M35 pcs · 23%
L35 pcs · 23%
XL30 pcs · 20%
XXL15 pcs · 10%
Total150 pcs · 100%

That is a real retail-size run: enough depth in M and L to sell through, enough spread to learn which size your customer actually buys. What it does not buy is a second wash, a second fabric or a second fit.

What 150 pieces buys, decision by decision

One style, one colour150 pieces. A full size run in one wash.
One style, two colours300 pieces. Colour choice, same silhouette.
Two styles, one colour each300 pieces. Two silhouettes, one wash standard.
Four styles, one colour each600 pieces. A small range, each style in one colour.

The trap for a first collection is spending the budget on silhouettes instead of colours, then discovering the fit was wrong in a colour nobody wanted. One style in one colour, sold and reordered, tells you more than four styles in one colour.

Where the money goes at this size

  • Sampling: USD 100 per style, 10–15 days, credited against the bulk invoice. At six styles that is USD 600 of work returned to you on the bulk order.
  • Tooling is one-off. Custom buttons, rivets or a branded label change the quotation once, not the price per piece.
  • Wash development is its own line. A one-step enzyme wash and a five-step vintage reproduction are different products; that is why wash is never folded into a single garment price.
  • Freight hurts small runs most. FOB Shenzhen means the goods are yours once loaded; a 150-piece shipment carries the same fixed costs as a 1,500-piece one, spread over a tenth of the units.

At our bands a 150-piece run lands roughly here: women's jeans from USD 13.97, men's jeans from USD 18, denim jackets USD 16.57–18.67, shirts USD 17.65–23.53, dresses and skirts USD 14–19. A costed quotation against your tech pack or reference garment is the only number that holds.

Why small batches cost more per piece

Three fixed constraints, none of them sewing speed:

Fabric dye lots. Mills sell by roll and by dye lot. A small dye lot is the real floor under any order; below it the mill will not open the loom, and buying from stock limits your wash options.

Cutting markers. A marker lays several sizes across the fabric at once. At 150 pieces you run one or two lays, which is still efficient; at 40 pieces the marker waste per garment rises sharply.

Wash batches. Our laundry runs stone, enzyme, laser and ozone in batches, and each batch has a capacity. A colourway smaller than a batch still consumes a batch — the mechanical reason the minimum is quoted per style per colour.

The longer explanation of those constraints is in what MOQ 150 actually means; this page is about the budget and the calendar.

The sampling calendar decides whether a first order lands in season. Two overlapping sample rounds plus one revision is 20–45 days — that is the part worth planning backwards from your launch date.

What to check before you commit

  • One style, one colour, one wash — and say which one out loud in the brief.
  • A size curve agreed in writing, with the size chart attached.
  • A reference garment or flat sketch rather than a photo from another brand.
  • Wash reference photos or a physical swatch; "medium blue" is not a specification.
  • The trim and label decision made before sampling, not after.
  • A delivery window and a destination port.

Missing most of that? Send the reference garment and the quantity. We can quote a band from that, and we will say which assumptions the band depends on.

Buyer questions
Is the 150-piece minimum per style or per order?

Per style per colour. Two colours of one style is 300 pieces; two styles in one colour is also 300 pieces.

Can I mix sizes within the 150 pieces?

Yes. Any size curve you set, as long as it totals 150 pieces per style per colour and the size chart is agreed before cutting.

Can I order two colours of the same style?

Yes, but that is 300 pieces in total, because the minimum applies per colour.

What does sampling cost and is it refundable?

USD 100 per style, 10-15 days. It is credited against the bulk invoice, so on a real order the sampling work is effectively included.

How long from first brief to delivery?

Sampling takes 10-15 days per round and bulk runs 30-35 days after sample approval and deposit, so a clean first order is roughly 8-11 weeks door to door, excluding freight transit.

Why is small-batch denim more expensive per piece?

Fabric dye lots, cutting marker waste and wash batch capacity are all fixed costs spread over fewer units. That is true of every denim factory, not a premium we add.
