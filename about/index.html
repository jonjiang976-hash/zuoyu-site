About

A denim factory in a denim town

Zuoyu Garment has been making denim in Dayong, Zhongshan since 2019. We are not a trading company with a factory page: sample room, cutting, sewing and laundry run on the same site.

What that means for a buyer: when a wash or a fit needs to change, the person who can change it is in the next building, not on a chat thread with a third party. It shortens the approval cycle, which is usually the biggest delay in a first order.

Where we are

6/F, No. 168, Yongheng Road, Qinggang Community, Dayong Town, Zhongshan, Guangdong, China. Dayong is one of China's recognised denim production clusters — the kind of town where the fabric mill, the laundry and the trims shop are all within a short drive. That density is not decoration: it is why a wash trial here takes days rather than an overseas round trip.

We have been on this site since 2019, and most of the people who approve a sample have been with the company for years. The merchandising team answers buyers in English; the sample room answers to the merchandising team.

How we work

Five working rules

  • Quote what can be made. If a design is not a fit for our lines, the quotation says so and names the reason.
  • Charge for samples, credit them back. A sample is work; we price it, then credit it against the bulk invoice.
  • Write down the no. Missing certifications are stated on the site, not discovered by your compliance team.
  • Keep the sealed standard. Every order's wash is approved against a physical reference kept on site.
  • Answer within one working day. During China working hours, Monday to Saturday, 09:00–18:00.
Who we work with

Brand owners, importers, wholesale distributors and e-commerce sellers in Europe, North America and Australia. Most of our customers are private-label brands of between two and forty styles per season — big enough to need a factory, small enough to need one that answers questions.

One company, two sales channels

This website, the womenswear store and the menswear store are the same legal entity: Zhongshan Zuoyu Garment Co., Ltd. The two Alibaba stores are our online sales channels; this site is the factory record. Prices, MOQ and lead times are the same on all three — if you find a difference, tell us and we will correct it.

Buyers often ask which store to use. Use the womenswear store for dresses, skirts, tops and women's jeans; use the menswear store for jeans, jackets, denim shirts, shorts and vests. If your order covers both, send one brief and we will quote it as a single order.
