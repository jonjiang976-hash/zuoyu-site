A denim factory in a denim town

Zuoyu Garment has been making denim in Dayong, Zhongshan since 2019. We are not a trading company with a factory page: sample room, cutting, sewing and laundry run on the same site.

Our story

A factory built around one fabric, not a catalogue of everything

Zuoyu Garment was registered in Zhongshan on 16 April 2019, in Dayong — a town in Guangdong that has been washing and sewing denim for decades. The name was chosen for a simple reason: the label inside a garment is the one thing a brand cannot outsource, and we wanted to be the company that makes the thing behind that label.

We made one deliberate decision early on and have kept it: denim only. Not denim plus knitwear, not denim plus accessories. Four categories — jeans, jackets, shirts and dresses — and the wash development that holds them together. Staying inside a single fabric is why a wash trial here takes days rather than an overseas round trip, and why the person who approves your sample can walk to the laundry instead of emailing a third party.

The second decision was about size. Our minimum is 150 pieces per style per colour because that is where fabric minimums, cutting markers and a wash batch start to behave predictably. It is a number built for first collections and small brands, not for container orders — those we can still run, but they are not who the factory was designed for.

The third decision is the one buyers notice last and rely on most: we publish what we do not hold. No OEKO-TEX Standard 100, no GRS, no amfori BSCI, no ISO 9001. What we do hold is a SMETA audit (Sedex reference ZAA600231250), a US CPSIA General Certificate of Conformity (BYT26080342) and a RoHS certificate (MTL24120600306R01). A supplier page that lists everything is easy to write and impossible to verify; a short list that survives a vendor questionnaire is worth more to both sides.

We would rather lose a quotation at the first email than lose a buyer at the inspection.

Today the site runs 2,300 m² of production and warehouse space with four sewing lines, an in-house laundry for stone, enzyme, laser and ozone finishing, two QC staff and two people in sampling and pattern development. Buyers reach us through this site or through our two Alibaba storefronts, and they get the same answer from the same team on either channel.

How we work

Five working rules

  • Quote what can be made. If a design is not a fit for our lines, the quotation says so and names the reason.
  • Charge for samples, credit them back. A sample is work; we price it, then credit it against the bulk invoice.
  • Write down the no. Missing certifications are stated on the site, not discovered by your compliance team.
  • Keep the sealed standard. Every order's wash is approved against a physical reference kept on site.
  • Answer within one working day. During China working hours, Monday to Saturday, 09:00–18:00.
Who we work with

Brand owners, importers, wholesale distributors and e-commerce sellers in Europe, North America and Australia. Most of our customers are private-label brands of between two and forty styles per season — big enough to need a factory, small enough to need one that answers questions.

One company, two sales channels

This website, the womenswear store and the menswear store are the same legal entity: Zhongshan Zuoyu Garment Co., Ltd. The two Alibaba stores are our online sales channels; this site is the factory record. Prices, MOQ and lead times are the same on all three — if you find a difference, tell us and we will correct it.

Buyers often ask which store to use. Use the womenswear store for dresses, skirts, tops and women's jeans; use the menswear store for jeans, jackets, denim shirts, shorts and vests. If your order covers both, send one brief and we will quote it as a single order.
